## Claims
| # | Claim in the guide | Sources and what they say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only a qualified person may submit building plans: a Professional Architect, a Professional Engineer or a registered building draughtsman | S1 s.70(2): "Any person who intends to erect any building shall cause to be submitted by a principal submitting person or submitting person ..."; S1 s.3: "'qualified person' means a Professional Architect, Professional Engineer or building draughtsman registered under any written law"; S2 by-law 3(2): "Every plan, drawing or calculation in respect of any building shall be submitted by a principal submitting person or submitting person"; S7: "PSP terdiri daripada Arkitek, jurutera profesional dan pelukis pelan berdaftar" |
| 2 | A registered Building Draughtsman is registered with the Board of Architects Malaysia (LAM) | S3 s.2 definition: a Building Draughtsman who "is registered with the Board"; S3 s.21 "Register of Building Draughtsmen"; S6 rule 25 |
| 3 | Building Draughtsman may submit plans only for buildings up to two storeys with total built-up floor area not over 300 m² | S3 s.24(5): "the Building Draughtsman shall be entitled to submit to the Building Authority for approval of any plans of buildings not exceeding two-storeys in height providing that the total built-up floor area does not exceed three hundred square metres in any one or series of project in the same development by the same client"; S5 clause 25(b) same text; S4 s.24(5) same text |
| 4 | 300 m² is about 3,230 sq ft | Arithmetic: 300 × 10.7639 = 3,229.2 sq ft |
| 5 | Only to the councils named in their registration / approved areas are written on the certificate | S3 s.24(1)(a): "he may submit plans only to, and within the limits of, the Building Authority for which area his application for registration has been approved"; S6 rule 25: "approved areas of operation within the limits of the building Authorities specified in the certificate of registration in respect of which a Building Draughtsman may submit plans" |
| 6 | The Board stopped registering new Building Draughtsmen in 2015 | S5 explanatory statement para 17: "Clause 23 seeks to delete section 22 of Act 117 to discontinue new registration of Building Draughtsmen"; S3 s.22(1) "(Repealed by Act A1480)" |
| 7 | Those practising today were registered (or deemed registered) on or before 31 Dec 2015; definition = registered on or before 1 June 2015, or registered/deemed registered on or before 31 Dec 2015 | S3 s.2 definition (a) "on or before 1 June 2015, is registered with the Board ..."; (b) "on or before 31 December 2015, is registered or deemed to be registered with the Board"; S4 same definition; S5 clause 23(2)–(3) |
| 8 | Those already registered may keep practising and renew every year | S5 clause 23(2)(b): "he shall continue to practise until the expiry of his certificate of registration which on application may be renewed annually for a period of one year upon payment of the prescribed fee ..."; S3 (transitional text reproduced after s.22) same words |
| 9 | Change made by the Architects (Amendment) Act 2015 (Act A1480), which deleted the registration section | S3 s.2 definition: "section 22 which has been deleted in subsection 23(1) of the Architects (Amendment) Act 2015 [Act A1480]"; S5 clause 23(1): "The principal Act is amended by deleting section 22" |
| 10 | Section 20: no one may practise as or call themselves a Building Draughtsman unless registered | S3 s.20: "No person shall, unless he is a Building Draughtsman— (a) practise or carry on business as a Building Draughtsman; (aa) be entitled to describe himself or hold himself out under any name, style or title— (i) bearing the words 'Building Draughtsman' or the equivalent thereto in any other language ..."; S4 same |
| 11 | The size limit counts one project or a series of projects in the same development by the same client, so a bigger house can't be split into smaller submissions for the same owner | S3 s.24(5) "in any one or series of project in the same development by the same client" (the guide's "cannot be split" is a plain reading of that phrase) |
| 12 | Not under a limited company; a person employed by a limited company, building contractor, manufacturer or commercial firm cannot be registered | S3 s.24(3): "No person who is employed by a limited company, building contractor, manufacturer or commercial firm shall be entitled to be registered under this Part"; s.24(4): "No Building Draughtsman shall practise under the name of a limited company or under the protection of limited liability" |
| 13 | Rules: must not sign drawings, plans or specifications for an approval unless they, their employees or people under their supervision prepared them | S6 Second Schedule Part Two (Code of Conduct for Building Draughtsmen, applied by rule 28(3)) para 3(8): "A Building Draughtsman shall not certify, affix or put his signature to drawings, plans or specifications which are not prepared by him, his employees or persons under his supervision for the purpose of obtaining the approval from the relevant Authority" |
| 14 | Architect is entitled to submit plans or drawings to any person or authority in Malaysia | S3 s.8(1): "Subject to sections 7A and 7B, only an Architect or a foreign architect who is residing in Malaysia for not less than one hundred and eighty days in any one calendar year shall be entitled to submit plans or drawings to any person or authority in Malaysia"; S4 same |
| 15 | The two-storey, 300 m² and council-area limits in s.24 apply to Building Draughtsmen, not Architects | S3 s.24 heading "Restrictions on Building Draughtsmen" and s.24(1) "A Building Draughtsman shall be subject to the following restrictions"; no such restriction in Part II/III for Architects |
| 16 | Only a registered Architect may use the title "Architect" and "Ar." | S3 s.7: "No person shall, unless he is an Architect – ... (aa) ... bearing the words 'Architect' ...; (ba) be entitled to describe himself as an 'Architect' and to use the abbreviation 'Ar.' before his name" |
| 17 | Guide covers Peninsular Malaysia (Act 133 applies there) | S1 s.1(2): "This Act shall apply only to Peninsular Malaysia." |
| 18 | "Pelukis pelan" in everyday Malay = someone who draws building plans | Plain meaning (pelukis = one who draws, pelan = plan); framing statement; S7 uses "pelukis pelan berdaftar" for the registered kind |
| 19 | Ar. Hidi Lau Wei Lin leads the firm and is an Architect registered with LAM | C1 About.tsx: "Ar. Hidi Lau Wei Lin is a practicing Architect registered with Lembaga Arkitek Malaysia (LAM)"; "The practice was established by Ar. Hidi Lau Wei Lin" |
