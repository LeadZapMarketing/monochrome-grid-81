## Claims (new or changed)
| # | Claim in the guide | Sources and what they say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In construction and property in Malaysia, CCC stands for Certificate of Completion and Compliance | S2 s.70(20) "certificate of completion and compliance"; S1 title "Certificate of Completion and Compliance (CCC)"; S3 by-law 25 |
| 2 | A registered building draughtsman may only submit plans for buildings of up to two storeys and up to 300 m² total built-up area | S5 s.24(5): "plans of buildings not exceeding two-storeys in height providing that the total built-up floor area does not exceed three hundred square metres"; Bill clause 25(b) same text |
| 3 | Under s.70(21) of Act 133 the PSP, before issuing the CCC, supervises the construction and ensures (a) built to approved plans, (b) completed in line with the council's technical conditions, (c) safe and fit for occupation | S2 s.70(21)(a)–(c): "supervise the erection of the building to ensure that the erection is in conformity with the approved plans ..."; "all technical conditions imposed by the local authority has been duly complied with"; "ensure that the building is safe and fit for occupation"; S1 (same three duties, already cited on the page) |
| 4 | The council may inspect the building works at any stage | S2 s.70(22): nothing in the Act affects the council's powers "pertaining to the erection and construction of a building for the purpose of ensuring that the erection and construction of such building are in conformity with the approved plans"; S3 by-law 25(5): "Nothing contained in this by-law shall prevent the local authority or any officer authorised by it ... from inspecting any building works at any stage thereof" |
| 5 | If work does not follow the approved plans or the by-laws, the council can order the PSP in writing to put it right, and direct the PSP to withhold the CCC until fixed | S2 s.70(23)(a) "a notice in writing, requiring compliance within the period specified ... in order that the noncompliance be rectified"; s.70(23)(b) "a directive in writing to withhold the issuance of the certificate of completion and compliance until such non-compliance has been rectified"; S3 by-law 25(5) "giving notice in writing to the principal submitting person ... ordering such failure or non-compliance to be rectified"; 25(6) "a directive in writing to the principal submitting person to withhold the issuance of the certificate of completion and compliance"; 25(14) CCC "shall not be issued ... until all the failure or non-compliance ... has been satisfactorily rectified" |
| 6 | If not fixed, the council may carry out the work itself and the building owner bears the cost | S2 s.70(24): "the local authority may itself cause any work to be executed or any measure to be taken"; s.70(25): "The cost ... shall be borne by the owner of the building"; S3 by-law 25(12) and 25(13) same |
| 7 | Eight of the 21 Form G need a letter or certificate from an outside agency (G8, G9, G11, G13, G14, G15, G16, G17) | S3 by-law 25(15)–(20) (each paragraph below); count: G8, G9, G11, G13, G14, G15, G16, G17 = 8 |
| 8 | G8 and G9: Bomba fire safety certificate; residential buildings up to 18 m high do not need it | S3 by-law 25(15): "A certificate of fire safety from the Fire and Rescue Department (except for residential buildings of not exceeding 18 metres in height) shall be attached with Forms G8 and G9" [Amendment 2021]; the same exception is printed on Forms G8 and G9 in the S3 schedule ("which are not exceeding 18 metres in height) shall be attached with this form"). Single document (the law text itself); the guide states it as the by-law's wording |
| 9 | G11: DOSH certificate of fitness | S3 by-law 25(16): "A certificate of fitness from the Department of Occupational Safety and Health shall be attached with Form G11" |
| 10 | G13: water authority confirms the supply is ready for connection | S3 by-law 25(17): "A letter of confirmation from the water authority that the water supply is ready for connection shall be attached with Form G13" |
| 11 | G14 and G15: sewerage clearance | S3 by-law 25(18): "A letter of clearance from a sewerage certifying agency shall be attached with Forms G14 and G15" |
| 12 | G16: TNB confirms electricity supply ready for connection | S3 by-law 25(19): "A letter of confirmation from Tenaga Nasional Berhad that the electrical power supply is ready for connection shall be attached with Form G16" |
| 13 | G17: council or JKR confirmation; treated as given if not received within 14 days of application | S3 by-law 25(20): "A letter of confirmation from the relevant local authority or Public Works Department shall be attached with Form G17 and if the letter of confirmation has not been received by the submitting person within fourteen days from the date of application, the letter of confirmation shall be deemed to be given" |
| 14 | Occupying, or letting someone occupy, without a CCC is an offence: fine up to RM250,000, up to 10 years' jail, or both (s.70(27)) | S2 s.70(27)(f): "occupies or permits to be occupied any building or any part thereof without a certificate of completion and compliance, shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding ten years or to both"; S4 confirms s.70(27)(f) and RM250,000 |
| 15 | The same penalty applies to anyone who issues a CCC without being the PSP, without the required forms, or against the council's direction to withhold it | S2 s.70(27)(a) "is not the principal submitting person but issues a certificate of completion and compliance"; (b) "issues ... without the relevant forms as prescribed"; (c) "issues ... in contravention of a direction given by the local authority to withhold such issuance"; the penalty sentence closes the whole subsection (a)–(f) |
| 16 | CF was issued by the council; since the CCC system began in 2007 buildings get a CCC from the PSP; projects already on the CF system could still be given a CF by the council | S1: "Bolehkah PBT masih mengeluarkan Perakuan Kelayakan Menduduki (CFO) walaupun sistem CCC telah dilaksanakan sejak tahun 2007?" answer: Act A1286 transitional note "membenarkan pengecualian bagi pembangunan yang sedang menggunakan sistem CFO ... PBT boleh mengeluarkan CFO"; S1 "Hanya ... PSP yang layak untuk mengeluarkan CCC, bukan Pihak Berkuasa Tempatan"; S2 s.70(20) |
| 17 | A renovation needs a CCC only for work under approved building plans (e.g. extension, structural changes); for such work the PSP supervises and issues the CCC | S6 claim 23 (approved): "For work under approved plans, the PSP supervises and issues the CCC" (S2 s.70(20), S1); extension/structural changes as plan-approval work per the approved renovation guide claims |
